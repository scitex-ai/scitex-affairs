Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-03-12

## Purpose

SciTeX is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and follows a dual-licensing model:

- Researchers and individuals: free under AGPL-3.0.
- Enterprises and institutions: a commercial license may be offered.

The CLA preserves the project's ability to sustain that model while keeping
the contributor's copyright.

The CLA Assistant will ask you to sign on your first pull request. You may
sign by commenting:

> I have read and agree to the SciTeX CLA.

Questions may be raised in the repository issue tracker.
