SciTeX Affairs
==============

``scitex-affairs`` keeps business and personal affairs in one validated place:
service catalogs and prices, append-only actual observations, optional
time-aware scenarios, and the pandas reports, plots and app views derived
from them. It is part of the `SciTeX <https://scitex.ai>`_ ecosystem.

.. code-block:: bash

   pip install scitex-affairs[all]
