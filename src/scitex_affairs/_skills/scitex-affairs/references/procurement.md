# Japanese public procurement screening

Use this workflow to find and assess paid public-sector research, software,
and data-service contracts. Obtain the applicant's current qualifications,
regions, evidence of corporate experience, capacity, and exclusions from their
private records. Do not assume a particular grade, company, or business stage.

Search the requested period in Japan time. Combine cross-agency indexes with
the issuing institution's procurement pages and open quotation/proposal routes.
Use [sources](procurement/sources.md) and [keywords](procurement/keywords.md)
as starting points; verify current official pages rather than assuming these
links or historical notices establish current availability.

For each candidate, inspect the notice, instructions, specification, corrections,
and Q&A. Keep document-request, participation, question, quotation, bid, and
performance deadlines separate, with source URL and evidence. Preserve date-only
values without inventing a time. An index deadline or listing end date is not
proof of the participation deadline. Record retrieval failures and unknowns.

Apply [eligibility and delivery gates](procurement/gates.md) before ranking.
Separate corporate contract experience from individual research experience.
A common government qualification does not prove an institution accepts it;
check its own registration, technical review, and document-release requirements.

Estimate scope, effort, travel, hardware/licenses, subcontracting, acceptance,
payment timing, IP/OSS constraints, and uncertainty before assessing margin.
Do not infer a budget from unrelated contract awards. Exclude failed gates and
expired mandatory deadlines from the actionable shortlist; retain them separately
when useful. Keep unresolved gates visible rather than treating them as passed.

Adapt the [report template](procurement/report-template.md) to the request.
Report verified candidates, watch items, rejected opportunities with reasons,
changes since the previous run, evidence gaps, and concrete next actions. Return
fewer candidates when fewer qualify. RFI and grant calls are separate from paid
contracts. A report cadence does not establish an installed recurring task.

For new specifications or answers, explain changed requirements, gate results,
price/effort risks, Go / Conditional Go / No Go reasoning, and concise draft
questions where needed. Prepare submissions and correspondence for review;
external sending follows the user's authorization for that action.
