# Business contest applications

Read the current official call, rules, application form, judging criteria, and
corrections. Check entrant eligibility, incorporation/location conditions,
prior-award restrictions, public disclosure, IP, attendance, and every stage's
deadline. Preserve original forms and keep draft versions separate from the
immutable record of what was actually submitted.

Map judging criteria to the applicant's evidence: problem, beneficiaries,
working product, differentiation, customer route, team, costs, and milestones.
Use the private company context for facts and the event brief for the ask.
Keep ambitions, forecasts, founder experience, corporate sales, and confirmed
customers distinct. Do not invent traction, selection, awards, or revenue.

For a pitch, use [presentation guidance](presentations.md) with the event's
required template and time limit. Rehearse the spoken timing and prepare Q&A
on evidence gaps and business assumptions. Identify the current working version
and the submitted version explicitly; highest version number alone is not proof
of submission or operator approval.

Report remaining requirements, draft artifacts, confirmed deadlines with primary
sources, and the next decision. Submission and messages to organizers require
authorization for the actual sending action; preparing a reviewable draft does
not establish a scheduled reminder or unattended monitoring service.
